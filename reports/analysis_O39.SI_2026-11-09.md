- *Loans* S$372bn (+14% YoY cc, +3% QoQ); FY26 guidance upgraded again: loan growth low-double-digit, income ≥ mid-single-digit growth, NII flat-to-slightly-down (from "down"), credit costs cut to 15–20bp from 20–25bp.

- *Rising-rate backdrop:* management assumes one more Fed hike in 4Q26, SORA ~1.2%. Rates rising hurts GEH marks near-term but helps NIM.

**Capital generation.** Fully-phased CET1 ~14% target (14.0% at 2Q26; 3Q26 figure not retrieved). Payout 50% ordinary; the remaining S$2.5bn capital-return programme is targeted for completion by FY2026 (special dividend/buyback with the final dividend — an identifiable 1Q27 catalyst). No equity raising expected. Cost-income 40.8% (3Q) / 39.2% (9M), opex flat YoY.

## 6. Final Verdict

**HOLD (lean accumulate on confirmation).** OCBC has the cleanest fundamental trend of the three — record banking-operations profit, second guidance upgrade, falling credit costs, lowest NPL (0.8%), and a capital-return completion in view — and it has de-rated ~10% since August. But the 3Q headline is dragged by GEH volatility, the P/B (2.03x vs 1.18x average) is still the richest in the group relative to history, no volume/flow confirmation is available, and base-case R:R is 0.8:1. Accumulate on a volume-backed close above S$30; the bull-case 2.6:1 is the closest any of the three comes to the 3:1 bar.
