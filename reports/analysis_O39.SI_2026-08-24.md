**The inflection**: this is not a smooth compounding line. ROE stagnated at 13.7% for two straight years (FY2023–FY2024), then *fell* to 12.6% in FY2025 as NIM compression (rate-cutting cycle) outpaced balance-sheet growth — a genuine efficiency stall, not just noise. The real inflection shows up in 2026: ROE troughed at 13.0% in 1Q26 and then jumped 1.4ppt q/q to 14.4% in 2Q26, a post-stagnation high last seen in the 2023 upcycle. Crucially, the driver is *not* rates (NIM kept compressing, 1.92%→1.70% y/y in 2Q) but a genuine mix-shift toward fee-based, capital-light income — the definition of an efficiency inflection rather than a cyclical rate tailwind. [Growbeansprout](https://growbeansprout.com/ocbc-2q26-profit-dividend)

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

### Value-trap check

- **NPL ratio: 0.9%, unchanged for eight consecutive quarters**, lowest of the three SG banks.
- **Total NPA coverage: 163%.**
- **Total allowances fell 28% q/q to S$156m in 2Q26**; **credit costs 14bp annualised**, down from 23bp in 1Q26.
- NPAs rose 4.1% q/q to S$3.132bn in absolute terms (small base, watch item). [The Singaporean Investor](https://www.thesingaporeaninvestor.sg/2026/08/07/oversea-chinese-banking-corporation-limited-sgx-o39-2q-1h-fy2026-results-review/)
- The one real headwind, **NIM compression** (1.92%→1.70% y/y 2Q; 1.98%→1.73% 1H), is **cyclical** (global rate-cutting), not structural credit deterioration; OCBC is hedging exposure — disclosed sensitivity has fallen to **~S$4m NII per bp** (~S$400m per 100bp/yr) from S$6-7m/bp in FY2023. This reads as a genuine GARP set-up, not a value trap.

### Hidden assets screen

- **Excess CET1 capital**: ~5.0ppt buffer above the 9.0% D-SIB minimum (quantified in Section 1).
- **Great Eastern Holdings stake**: OCBC holds **~88–94%** of GEH. GEH embedded value **rose 11.5% to S$20.1bn in FY2025**; NBEV +19% y/y to S$740m (48.2% margin). [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/asia/news/breaking-news/great-eastern-fy25-profit-hits-record-nbev-increases-566425.aspx) **Privatization status**: OCBC's S$0.9bn exit offer at S$30.15/share **failed** the shareholder vote (63.5% support vs. 75% threshold required) and has **lapsed**. [OCBC media release](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2025/ocbc-backs-great-eastern-delisting-proposal); [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/asia/news/breaking-news/great-eastern-to-relist-after-failed-ocbc-delisting-bid-541978.aspx) GEH's fallback is a 1-for-1 bonus + Class C non-voting share issue to restore free float (diluting OCBC's stake toward ~88%); GEH shares remain **suspended since 15 July 2024** with **no confirmed resumption date** — a live, unscheduled overhang/catalyst.
- **Bank of Singapore**: AUM **>USD145bn as of 3Q2025**, up ~20% since early 2023; HK centre already exceeded its 2024–2026 AUM growth target. [Hubbis](https://www.hubbis.com/news/bank-of-singapore-targets-asia-s-top-five-private-banks-with-usd145-billion-aum) No standalone valuation disclosed — **data not found**.
- **Property/premises revaluation surplus**: **data not found** in this search pass — not broken out in the summarized results materials surfaced.

### 2-stage DDM sanity check
Base D₀ = S$1.00 (est. FY26E ordinary dividend, ~50% payout on ~S$2.00 FY26E EPS run-rate; special dividend excluded from perpetuity, treated as a bonus).

- **Conservative**: Ke 8.5%, 5-yr growth 4%, terminal growth 2.5% → PV(Y1-5) ≈ S$4.41 + PV(TV) ≈ S$13.83 → **Fair value ≈ S$18.25**
- **Aggressive**: Ke 7.5%, 5-yr growth 7% (≈ ROE 13.5% × 50% retention), terminal growth 3.5% → PV(Y1-5) ≈ S$4.93 + PV(TV) ≈ S$25.29 → **Fair value ≈ S$30.20**

Current price (S$30.97) sits **above even the aggressive DDM case**. Justified-P/B cross-check: (ROE−g)/(Ke−g) at ROE 13.5%/Ke 8.5%/g 3.5% ≈ 2.0x (below current 2.36x); only Ke 7.5%/g 4.0% reaches ≈2.71x. **The stock is pricing in close to the best-case growth/cost-of-equity combination — little valuation cushion remains.**

## 5. Execution & Stress Test

- Base case upside: (31.60−30.97)/30.97 = **+2.0%**
- Bull case upside: (37.23−30.97)/30.97 = **+20.2%**
- Downside to stop (S$28.63): (30.97−28.63)/30.97 = **-7.6%**
- **Base-case R:R ≈ 0.26:1 — fails the 3:1 bar decisively.**
- Bull-case R:R ≈ 20.2/7.6 ≈ **2.66:1 — still short of 3:1.**
- Using deeper SMA50 stop (S$27.44, -11.4%): bull R:R ≈ 1.77:1 — worse.

**Stress scenarios**:
1. **Renewed rate cuts**: ~S$4m NII/bp sensitivity → a further 50bp of cuts ≈ **~S$200m annualised NII hit** (~3% of FY25 net profit), partially offset by loan volume growth.
2. **Regional/China-ASEAN credit shock**: asset quality is currently a strength (0.9% NPL, 163% coverage) versus UOB's flagged China real-estate exposure, but OCBC's growing wealth-fee income (41% of revenue) is more market/risk-appetite sensitive than pure NII, so a risk-off shock would hit the very catalyst driving 2026 growth.
3. **Broad market/index selloff**: ~54–55% STI weight for the big 3 banks plus a P/B re-rated to 2.36x (vs. 1.16x historical) creates real mean-reversion risk independent of fundamentals, consistent with the bear-case target zone (S$24–28).
4. **Great Eastern overhang**: failed delisting vote, pending free-float-restoration share issuance, no confirmed relisting date — an unresolved, unscheduled binary event risk.

## 6. Final Verdict

**Hold**

OCBC's fundamentals are genuinely inflecting — ROE recovered from a two-year 13.7% stagnation and a 12.6% FY2025 trough to 14.4% in 2Q26, driven by a real, non-rate catalyst (wealth management + Great Eastern insurance, now 41% of group revenue and growing 27–51% y/y), asset quality shows no crack (0.9% NPL, 163% coverage), and a 14.0% CET1 ratio against a 9.0% minimum funds both growth and the S$2.5bn capital-return programme without dilution. That is a legitimate quality-and-growth story. But the price has already caught up to it: P/B has re-rated from a 1.16x historical average to 2.36x, the 2-stage DDM's aggressive-case fair value (S$30.20) sits at/below spot (S$30.97), and the analyst consensus target (S$30.96) has converged onto the current quote. The resulting base-case risk/reward of ~0.26:1 (2% upside to the nearest credible target vs. 7.6% downside to key technical support) fails the 3:1 bar by a wide margin, and even the bull case falls just short at 2.66:1. This is a real improving business trading at a price that already reflects the improvement — better suited to a buy-the-dip watchlist (toward S$27.44–28.63) or a post-GEH-resolution re-entry than a fresh position at current levels.
