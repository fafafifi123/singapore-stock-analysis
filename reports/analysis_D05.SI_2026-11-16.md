**Trend: dip, then recovery, then a stall.** The 2Q26 inflection toward 18% was interrupted by 3Q26. Net profit was S$2.81bn (-5% YoY), against consensus of S$2.93–2.95bn, because markets trading income fell 65% to S$152m. Pre-allowance profit was a record and expenses fell 1%, so earnings quality is intact. ROIC is not meaningful for a bank, so ROE is the capital-efficiency measure.

**Catalysts for 4Q26/1Q27:**
- **Exit NIM 1.90%**, above the 1.87% 3Q average, and SGD rates are now rising (Fed hike cycle, SORA up from 1.06% in 2Q to 1.32%). NIM upside is the main 4Q driver.
- **Trading income should mean-revert.** 3Q's S$152m compares with ~S$430m a quarter in 1H26, so any normalisation is pure upside to 4Q.
- **Wealth:** AUM is a record S$544bn, and fees are guided to grow low double digits in FY27.
- **FY27 guidance:** NII growth just below 5%, cost/income low-40s, specific provisions 17–20bp. The 4Q26 results and FY27 targets come in Feb 2027 (outside the 4Q window, inside the 6-month horizon).

**Capital generation (used instead of FCF yield).** CET1 is 16.1% transitional / 14.1% fully phased-in after the MAS multiplier cut, against a ~9% requirement. Dividends are S$0.81 a quarter (S$3.24 annualised; ~78% of annualised 9M EPS of ~S$4.14), all funded organically. About 2.6bn of the 3bn buyback is still unused. The **capital-return yield is ~4.7%** at S$69.60. CET1 drifted down from 16.6%, which is by design.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

**Value-trap check: cyclical, not structural.** The NPL ratio is **0.9%** (2Q26: 1.0%) and specific allowances are 17bp. Coverage is 134%, or 202% including collateral. NIM fell from 2.13% (FY24) to 1.87% (3Q26) with the rate cycle, and that cycle has now turned: the Fed is hiking and SORA is rising. Loans are +11% YoY and deposits +10%. The miss came from volatile trading income, not from the franchise. A value trap would show falling NII, rising NPLs or a shrinking deposit base, and none of these is present.

**Hidden assets.** There is no cash pile or land bank larger than the market cap (~S$197bn). The optionality lies in two places:
- **SRCB stake:** a 19.9% stake in Shenzhen Rural Commercial Bank, at a cost of ~S$1.8–2.0bn (~S$0.65–0.70/share).
- **Excess CET1:** ~500bp+ above the requirement on a phased-in basis (more than S$15bn on ~S$330bn RWA; derived). This could fund more capital return beyond 2027.

Premises revaluation surplus: data not found.

*Band method:* the bands come from DBS's Annual Report 2025 five-year summary, FY2021–25. P/E is on the average annual price: 11.3, 10.6, 8.3, 9.0, 12.4. P/B is on year-end book value: 1.4, 1.6, 1.4, 1.5, 2.0. There are only five annual points, so the bands are indicative. The mean ±1 SD bands translate to **S$32–45 on P/B** and **S$36–50 on P/E**.

The re-rating is partly structural. ROE in 2021–22 was ~11–12%, against ~17% now, so the old mean understates fair value. Even so, the stock prices in sustained high ROE plus capital return. *EV/EBITDA is not meaningful for a bank* because interest expense is an operating cost: deposits are raw material, not financing, so "EV" and "EBITDA" have no economic meaning.

**Justified P/B and 2-stage DDM** (BVPS S$24.4, growing ~4% a year):
- **Justified P/B** = (ROE − g)/(COE − g):
  - Conservative: ROE 15%, g 3%, COE 9% → 2.0x, **~S$49**.
  - Base: 17% / 3% / 8.5% → 2.55x, **~S$62**.
  - Aggressive: 18% / 3.5% / 7.5% → 3.6x, **~S$88**.
- **2-stage DDM** at COE 8.5%. Stage 1: S$3.24 in FY26–27, then S$2.64 ordinary growing 5% to year 5. Stage 2: exit on year-5 BVPS of ~S$29.7.
  - Conservative exit at 2.0x P/B: **~S$52**.
  - Base exit at 2.55x: **~S$62**.
  - Aggressive exit at 3.2x: **~S$75**.
- **Fair-value range: S$49–88, central ~S$62–75.** At S$69.60, the price sits between the base and aggressive cases. The market is not cheap, but it no longer prices the full bull case.

**6-month target: S$74** (unchanged). Base range S$72–76. The bull case is **S$79**: back to the 52-week high on 4Q trading recovery plus exit NIM. The bear case is **S$64**: de-rating toward the conservative DDM. Total return to base is +6.3%, plus ~S$1.62 in dividends (~2.3%), for **≈ +8.6%**.

**Sentiment:** pre-results broker targets average ~S$83, 20% above the price, and no post-results revisions were found. Expect trims toward S$75–80, but note that no broker downgrade has been reported. Retail investors were net buyers of SGX stocks in October (+S$1.65bn) while institutions sold.

## 5. Execution & Stress Test

| Scenario | Entry | Target | Stop | Upside | Downside | R:R |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base | S$69.60 | S$74.00 | S$66.40 | +6.3% | -4.6% | **1.4 : 1** |
| Bull | S$69.60 | S$79.00 | S$66.40 | +13.5% | -4.6% | **2.9 : 1** |
| Dip entry | S$68.30 | S$74.00 | S$66.40 | +8.3% | -2.8% | **3.0 : 1** |

**Hard stop: S$66.40**, i.e. below the SMA200 (S$66.65) by ~0.2x ATR. A close below it would break the long-term uptrend. **At spot, the 3:1 rule is not met** (1.4:1 base, 2.9:1 bull). The rule is met only on an entry at ≤S$68.30 (base) or ≤S$69.50 (bull target).

**Stress tests:**
1. **Rate cuts / SORA reverses:** less likely now that the Fed is hiking, but a US slowdown after the midterms could flip it. A return of SORA to ~1.0% against the 1.6% plan would cut FY27 NII by ~5% and EPS by ~5–8%. Fair P/B then falls to ~2.4x, or **~S$59** (-15%).
2. **Regional credit shock (China/GBA/ASEAN):** specific provisions rise from 17bp to 50bp, an extra ~S$1.5bn a year and ~-12% EPS. SRCB impairment is a risk. The CET1 buffer absorbs it, but buybacks and capital-return continuity would be questioned. Valuation falls to the conservative case: **~S$52–55** (-22%).
3. **Broad market sell-off:** the STI is already below its SMA200. A de-rating to the +1 SD P/B band (1.83x) gives **S$45** (-35%), the tail case. A more realistic sector multiple of 2.3x gives ~S$56 (-20%). The 4.7% yield and the buyback are the main support.

## 6. Final Verdict

**HOLD (accumulate on dips to S$68.3 or below).** The franchise is unchanged and arguably improving at the margin:
- NPL is 0.9%.
- Exit NIM is 1.90% and the rate backdrop has turned supportive.
- CET1 is far above requirements, and capital return now looks extendable beyond 2027.

The stock is oversold (RSI 28.6) and at the bottom of its 20-day band. Against that:
- Volume and flows still show **distribution** (up/down volume 0.67, falling OBV, October institutional outflows).
- Valuation sits at +4 to +5 SD of the old five-year bands.
- R/R at spot is 1.4:1 base, short of the 3:1 rule.

**Upgrade to Speculative Buy** in either of two cases:
- a tested hold of S$68–69 with the 4Q trading rebound in view (R/R ≥3:1), or
- a volume-confirmed close above S$72.4, then S$75.1.

**Exit or reassess** on a close below S$66.40.
