**ROE trend:** the FY25 dip has reversed, and 9M26 ROE is above FY23–24. **Banking Operations profit hit a record S$1.90bn (+16% YoY).** The GEH contribution fell 58% YoY in 3Q to S$147m on unrealised MTM bond losses, which Phillip expects to reverse as the bonds mature.

**Catalysts, next two quarters:**
- **NIM has bottomed** (management: exit NIM 1.67%, 3Q 1.66%). Higher US rates should lift NII as USD assets reprice. The 10-year US yield rising from 4.86% (6 Nov) to ~5.15% strengthens this, but it also adds to MTM losses on GEH's bond book in 4Q. That is a new near-term negative.
- **Loans:** S$372bn, +14% YoY, the fastest of the three banks. FY26 guidance was raised: loans low double digits, income at least mid-single-digit growth, credit costs 15–20bp.
- **4Q26 results and final dividend (Feb 2027).** The capital-return programme completes in FY26.

**Capital generation (in place of FCF yield):** ROE of 13.5% at a 50% payout gives ~6.7% organic book growth. Fully phased CET1 is 14.0% (the operating target) despite 14% loan growth, so there is no dilution. The earnings yield is 7.0% (1/14.3x) and the dividend yield is ~3.8% on S$0.99 consensus DPS. Cost-to-income for 9M is 39.2%.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

**Value-trap check: cyclical, not structural.** NPL is **0.8%** (lowest of the three) with **168%** coverage and 6bp credit costs in 3Q. Loans are growing double digits and NIM has bottomed. The CASA ratio slipping to 48.4% (USD wholesale funding repriced first) is a watch item but not structural. Asset-turnover and inventory tests do not apply to a bank. The 8% fall this week came with no company-specific news, so it is a multiple de-rating, not a franchise problem.

**Hidden assets:**
- **Great Eastern (~93.7% economic interest):** OCBC's share of FY25 embedded value is ≈ S$18.8bn, about **S$4.2/share or ~16% of the ~S$118bn market cap** (derived). The market cap is lower this week, so this share rises.
- **Bank of Ningbo (20%):** the partnership was extended for 10 years in Sep 2026. OCBC's equity share of earnings is ~RMB3.3bn per half-year (derived). Stake market value: data not found.
- **Excess CET1:** 14.0% fully phased against a 9% minimum including the buffer. No cash pile exceeds market cap.

*Band method (unchanged):* year-end price/NAV for FY2021–25 (1.01, 1.10, 1.07, 1.13, 1.27; AR2025), giving a mean of 1.12x and an SD of 0.10x. The stock remains about **+8 SD** above the mean, a regime change driven by ROE rising from ~11% to 13.5%. **EV/EBITDA is not meaningful for a bank**, because deposits are raw material, not financing. P/B against ROE is the lens.

**Two-stage DDM / justified P/B (BVPS S$13.73, COE 8.5%):** Stage 1 assumes 3 years at 13.5% ROE and a 50% payout, giving BVPS S$16.70 and S$2.52 of PV dividends. Stage 2 is an exit multiple on that BVPS:
- *Conservative* exit at 1.5x → **S$22.1**.
- *Base* exit at 1.9x → **S$27.4** (+4.6% from spot). Cross-check: Phillip's 2.22x FY26E P/B gives S$30.50.
- *Aggressive* exit at 2.3x → **S$32.6**.
- **COE +50bp sensitivity (new):** base justified P/B (13.5% − 3.5%)/(9.0% − 3.5%) = 1.82x, or **~S$25.0**. Spot sits between the higher-COE value and the base DDM.
- **Fair-value range: S$22–33.** The price has moved from ~4% *above* base to ~4% *below* it.

**6-month target: S$29.0 (cut from S$29.5).** That is the midpoint of the SMA20 (S$28.17) and SMA50 (S$29.63), below the Phillip (S$30.50) and Minichart-reported (S$30.68) targets. The bull case is **S$32.5** (52-week high retest on the 4Q print and NIM upturn). The bear case is **S$23.5** (+50bp COE value less a further market leg). Return to base is +10.7%.

**Sentiment:** brokers are constructive (two targets around S$30.5–30.7), but momentum traders have unwound last week's "OCBC is the safe bank" rotation.

**New hard stop: S$24.50**, about 0.2 ATR below the July low of S$24.65. **Housekeeping:** last week's pullback entry (S$27.40) was filled on 18 Nov (low S$27.01) and stopped at S$26.90 on 20 Nov, a **-1.8% loss**. **At spot, the base case fails 3:1.** Only the bull case, or an entry at S$25.0–25.3, clears it.

**Stress tests:**
1. **Rate cuts instead of hikes (-20bp NIM):** ~S$1.1bn of NII lost (~-11% EPS), partly offset by GEH bond gains. ROE ~12% gives ~1.7x P/B: **~S$23–24 (-10%)**.
2. **Higher-for-longer yields (new):** NII benefits, but GEH MTM losses widen and COE rises 50bp: **~S$25 (-5%)**. Less damaging than for DBS because OCBC's P/B premium is smaller.
3. **Regional credit shock (China/HK property, Indonesia, tariffs):** credit costs rising from 6bp to 40bp means ~S$1.3bn of extra provisions (~-15% EPS). The best coverage of the three banks (168%) helps. **~S$23–24 (-10%)**.
4. **Broad market sell-off continues:** de-rating to 1.5x P/B gives **S$20.6 (-21%)**. A reversion to the +2 SD historical band (1.31x) gives S$18.0, the tail case.

## 6. Final Verdict

**HOLD. Accumulate only in the S$25.0–25.3 zone, or on a reclaim of S$27.42.** The fundamentals are still the best of the three banks:
- record banking-operations profit
- 13.5% ROE
- NPL 0.8% with 168% coverage
- 14% loan growth funded without dilution
- NIM bottoming, by management's account

Valuation is better than a week ago: 1.91x P/B and 14.3x P/E, about 4% below the DDM base. The tape is the issue:
- the S$26.90 stop and the 4 Nov low broke on 1.79x volume
- OBV and CMF have both rolled over
- the STI is in a rates-driven de-rating

Base-case R:R at spot is 1.6:1, below the 3:1 hurdle. Not a Value Trap and not yet a Gem. If the stock holds above the SMA200 or the S$24.65 July low while the market stabilises, it becomes the best risk/reward of the three banks. **Exit or reassess on a close below S$24.50.**
