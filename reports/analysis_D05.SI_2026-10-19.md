# DBS Group Holdings (D05.SI) — Hidden Gem Analysis

*Analysis date: 19 October 2026. Figures in S$ unless stated. Reference price: **S$74.80** (19 Oct 2026, 11:50 SGT, [Beansprout](https://growbeansprout.com/quote/D05.SI)). Latest reported results: 2Q26 (6 Aug 2026); **3Q26 results are due 5 Nov 2026** ([Kopi Notes](https://thekopinotes.com/articles/news/singapore-banks-october-2026-selloff/)).*

**Data caveat:** SGX does not publish broker-code ("bandarmology") or daily foreign net-flow series in any source I could reach, and live volume history was unavailable. Where data was not found it is stated, not estimated. Book value per share (~S$25.7) is back-solved from the cited P/B of 2.87x at ~S$73.85.

## 1. Quality & Growth Filter

**ROE inflection (3-year):** FY2023 18.0% → FY2024 18.0% → FY2025 16.2% (trough, higher global minimum tax and NIM compression) → 1Q26 17.0% → 2Q26 17.5% ([DBS 2Q26](https://www.dbs.com/newsroom/DBS_second_quarter_net_profit_up_9pct_to_record_SGD_3_08_billion_as_quarterly_total_income_crosses_SGD_6_billion_for_first_time), [Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/dbs-group-holdings-ltd-d05-q2-2026_FYKhlN9E)). Net profit hit a record S$3.08bn in 2Q26 (+9% YoY), total income crossed S$6bn for the first time. ROIC is not meaningful for a bank (deposits are raw material); ROE/ROTE is the efficiency metric.

**Catalyst, next two quarters:** fee income, not rates. Wealth fees and AUM (>S$500bn) are growing double digits; new wealth centres roll out from 3Q26. Management guided FY26 profit better than the earlier "slightly below 2025" but also expects NIM to ease further (2Q26 NIM 1.87%, down from 2.01% in FY25). The 5 Nov print is the test of whether fees offset margin.

**Cash strength (capital generation substitute for FCF):** CET1 ~16.6% transitional vs 9% MAS minimum; S$8bn capital-return programme (S$3bn buyback + S$0.15/quarter capital-return dividend through FY2027) funded organically, no dilution. FY26/27 dividend yield estimates 4.5% / 5.0% ([Beansprout](https://growbeansprout.com/dbs-ocbc-uob-share-price-oct-2026)).

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

- **Value-trap check:** not applicable in the classic sense. Asset quality is clean (NPL 1.0%, allowance charges ~15bps), loans +8% YoY. The weak point (NIM) is cyclical and decelerating, not structural.
- **Hidden assets:** ~19.9% stake in Shenzhen Rural Commercial Bank (~S$1.8–2.0bn cost; market value vs book not found) and excess CET1. Neither approaches market cap (~S$210bn). **No asset cushion; this is a quality compounder, not an asset play.**

**Valuation vs history:** DBS trades ~1.5x its 5-year average book multiple. The multiple, not the earnings, is the exposure.

**2-stage valuation (justified P/B × BVPS S$25.7):**
- Conservative (ROE 15%, g 3%, COE 9%): 2.0x → **~S$51**
- Aggressive (ROE 18%, g 4%, COE 7.5%): 4.0x → **~S$103**
- Dividend check: S$3.24 run-rate / (COE 8% − g 3.5%) ≈ S$72 midpoint.

The market is pricing roughly the midpoint-to-aggressive case.

**6-month target (to Apr 2027): S$78** base (+4.3%); bull S$85 (+13.6%, sustained wealth fees and stable yields); bear S$66 (−11.8%, multiple compression with a further bond-yield spike). Consensus upside quoted at ~29% looks stale versus the S$73–76 targets seen after 2Q26.

**Sentiment:** DBS fell ~6% over 7–8 Oct to ~S$73.85 (lowest since late July) on a global bank sell-off (KBW bank index −12.3% mid-Aug to 8 Oct), US 10-year yield 5.32%, a September Fed hike, and a Citi Sell call on OCBC. It has since recovered ~1.3%.

## 4. Bandarmology & Momentum

- **Accumulation/Distribution (60 days):** volume series not available. Price action only: new record in early Sept → ~−6% two-day flush 7–8 Oct → modest rebound. That is a distribution-then-shakeout pattern, not hidden accumulation. **No evidence of accumulation found.**
- **Foreign flow:** net buy/sell data not found. Banks are heavily foreign-owned and moved together, indicating sector-level, not stock-specific, flow.
- **Pivot (breakout confirmation):** reclaim of the pre-sell-off level **~S$78.5** (derived from the 6% drop to 73.85), above the S$77.97 August high cited previously. A close above ~S$78.5 on volume confirms the base is complete. Support: S$73.85 (8 Oct low), then ~S$69–70 (prior 50-day area, previous report).

## 5. Execution & Stress Test

- Entry S$74.80; Hard stop **S$69.5** (below the 50-day support zone; −7.1%, S$5.30 risk).
- Reward to base S$78: S$3.20 → **0.6:1**. Reward to bull S$85: S$10.20 → **1.9:1**. **Fails the 3:1 test.** A 3:1 setup needs entry near ~S$70–71 (target 78–85).

**Stress tests:**
1. *Further rate shock / yields up:* multiple compression toward 2.5x P/B implies ~S$64. DBS is the most multiple-sensitive of the three.
2. *Rate cuts resume (SORA down):* DBS's SGD book is liability-sensitive (~S$11m NII per bp); fee growth must offset.
3. *Regional credit event:* CET1 buffer of ~760bps absorbs it, but buybacks would pause; allowance charges could rise from 15bps toward 40bps+.
4. *Market sell-off:* ~26% STI weight; wealth fees are market-sensitive, so earnings and multiple weaken together.

## 6. Final Verdict

**HOLD.** Fundamentals are improving (ROE back to 17.5%, record profit, clean credit, self-funded capital return), but nothing is hidden or cheap at ~2.9x book. The October sell-off took the price back only to roughly where it sat in July, and risk/reward is ~1.9:1 at best. Accumulate below ~S$71; re-rate the call after the 5 Nov results. Breakout buy only on a close above ~S$78.5.
