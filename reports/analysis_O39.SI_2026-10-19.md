# Oversea-Chinese Banking Corporation (O39.SI) — Hidden Gem Analysis

*Analysis date: 19 October 2026. Figures in S$ unless stated. Reference price: **S$28.97** (19 Oct 2026, 11:50 SGT, [Beansprout](https://growbeansprout.com/quote/O39.SI)). Latest reported results: 2Q26 (early Aug 2026); **3Q26 results due 6 Nov 2026** ([Kopi Notes](https://thekopinotes.com/articles/news/singapore-banks-october-2026-selloff/)).*

**Data caveat:** broker-code and daily foreign net-flow data are not published for SGX in any source I could reach; volume history was unavailable. Missing items are marked "not found". BVPS (~S$14.95) is back-solved from the cited P/B of 1.94x at ~S$29.00.

## 1. Quality & Growth Filter

**ROE:** 2Q26 annualised ROE **14.4%** (1H26 13.7%), with net profit a record S$2.22bn (+22% YoY, first quarter above S$2bn) ([Beansprout](https://growbeansprout.com/ocbc-2q26-profit-dividend)). FY23–FY25 ROE series was not retrievable this run; the inflection is evidenced by the 1H26 profit of S$4.19bn (+13%) and the quarterly step-up. ROIC is not meaningful for a bank.

**Catalysts (next two quarters):**
- Guidance raised at 2Q26: loan growth to high-single/low-double digit (from mid-single), total income "to grow", cost-income in the low-40% range.
- Wealth fees a record S$470m (+45% YoY); net fee income +28%; insurance income +68% YoY (Great Eastern; NBEV margin 49.8% vs 44.7%). Non-interest income S$1.91bn (+51%).
- Headwind: NIM 1.70% (−22bps YoY, lowest of the three banks); net interest income still guided slightly lower.

**Cash strength:** 50% ordinary payout (interim 47c, +15%) plus an S$2.5bn capital-return plan targeted for completion in FY2026, no scrip dilution. CET1 not found this run (prior reports: comfortably above 14%). FY26/27 yield estimates 3.7% / 3.4%, the thinnest of the three.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

- **Value-trap check:** not a trap. NPL 0.9%, coverage 163%, credit-cost guide 20–25bps. The weakness is rate-cycle NIM.
- **Hidden assets:** the principal one is the Great Eastern stake (insurance embedded value; RHB argues integration is "not fully priced in"). Market value vs book for GEH and property holdings was not found, so no asset-cover claim is made. **Nothing indicates assets exceeding market cap (~S$130bn).**

## 4. Bandarmology & Momentum

- **Accumulation/Distribution (60 days):** volume data not found. Price action: record high >S$31–32 into early Oct, a high-velocity 10% two-day drop, then flat for 7 sessions. Flat tape after a flush is consistent with stabilisation but is **not** confirmation of accumulation.
- **Foreign flow:** not found. Banks are heavily foreign-held; the Citi downgrade was the trigger.
- **Pivot:** **~S$32** (pre-sell-off level). A close above ~S$31.5–32 on rising volume confirms the base. Support: S$28.5–29.0 (late-Aug level / 8 Oct close area), then S$27.5 (Citi target).

## 5. Execution & Stress Test

- Entry S$28.97; Hard stop **S$27.50** (−5.1%, S$1.47 risk).
- Reward: base S$31.5 → S$2.53 = **1.7:1**; bull S$33.7 → S$4.73 = **3.2:1**. The 3:1 test is passed only on the bull path.

**Stress tests:**
1. *Rates/yields rise further (US10Y already 5.32%):* OCBC has the thinnest dividend yield premium over the 10-year SGS (~0.7pp pre-sell-off, lowest since 2010); a de-rating to 1.75x P/B gives ~S$26.
2. *SORA falls, NIM → ~1.6%:* NII drag is the Citi thesis; offset needs wealth/insurance income growth to persist.
3. *Credit shock (Greater China/property):* NPL 0.9% and 163% coverage give the best buffer of the three, but credit cost could double from 20–25bps.
4. *Equity market sell-off:* wealth fees (63% of fee income) and Great Eastern investment income are market-linked, so the engine driving the beat is also the most exposed.

## 6. Final Verdict

**SPECULATIVE BUY (tactical, small size).** OCBC is the only one of the three with an upgraded guidance, record earnings momentum, and a sell-off-created entry with a defined stop (S$27.50, where the bearish Citi target sits). Bull-case risk/reward just clears 3:1, base case does not, so it is not a Strong Buy/Gem. Not cheap (~1.9x book, 3.7% yield) and the NIM debate is unresolved. Key checkpoint: 6 Nov results. Exit if S$27.50 breaks; add on a close above ~S$32.
