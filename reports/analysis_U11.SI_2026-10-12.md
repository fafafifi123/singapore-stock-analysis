## 1. Quality & Growth Filter

| Period | ROE | Note |
|---|---|---|
| FY2023 | 13.4% | peak |
| FY2025 | **9.6%** | trough: S$2.0bn pre-emptive general allowances, net profit −23% to S$4.7bn; NPL held 1.5% |
| 1H26 | 11.5% → ~11.8% | sequential recovery; management aims ~14% medium-term |

**Inflection:** FY2025 was a deliberate provisioning trough, not credit deterioration; operating profit held at S$7.7bn. With provisions normalised, earnings recover by arithmetic even without growth.

**Catalysts (next 2 qtrs):** (1) wealth fees +16% in 1H26 (ASEAN-4 ex-Citi +30%; 2Q26 net fees record S$665m); (2) the Fed hike helps — UOB is the most rate-sensitive of the three, so a higher-for-longer curve directly eases NIM pressure; (3) cost-income back toward low-40s (44.6% in FY25).

**Cash strength:** S$2bn buyback (2025–27), ~40% done by 2Q26 (≈S$1.2bn remaining ≈1.8% of market cap); CET1 held "slightly above 14%" after returns, 400+bp excess; no dilution. FY26E yield **4.4%**, FY27E **4.6%**.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

- **Value-trap check:** NPL 1.5% stable with 50% ordinary payout; weakness is cyclical (NIM, one-off provisioning), not structural. Asset-turnover/inventory not applicable to banks.
- **Hidden assets:** ASEAN franchise post-Citi (8.5m customers, ~S$1bn run-rate revenue ambition) is embedded optionality rather than a balance-sheet asset; no asset exceeds market cap.

Does not strictly reach 3:1; bull-case 3:1 entry is S$40.0 — essentially today's price — and the conservative fair value (≈S$39) sits just below entry, so the asymmetry is the best of the three.

**Stress tests:**
1. **Bank sell-off extends:** P/B to ~1.05x ⇒ ≈S$35 (−13%); yield >5% and the remaining buyback provide support.
2. **Fed back to cuts:** biggest NIM sensitivity of the three; ROE recovery stalls. S$35–38.
3. **Regional credit shock (Greater China property, ASEAN):** FY25's S$2bn buffer is already in the numbers, softening the hit; NPL 1.5% → 2%+ plausible. S$33–36.
Upside: rate-driven NIM relief + normalised provisions → ROE 12%+, S$45–48.

## 6. Final Verdict

**SPECULATIVE BUY** (scale in; small position; stop S$37.5). Cheapest multiple, ROE trough already behind it, rate path now supportive and an active buyback — but R:R is below 3:1 and the bank-sector sell-off is not over (global bank index still falling). Not a "Strong Buy" gem; not a value trap.
