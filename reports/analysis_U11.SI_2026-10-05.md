**Inflection:** a sequential recovery from the FY2025 air-pocket, but ROE remains well below FY23–24 and below DBS/OCBC. ROIC replaced by ROE for a bank.

**Catalysts (next 2 quarters):** (1) Fed hike: 43.3% SGD loans and 66% of revenue from NII make UOB the biggest beneficiary, though lagged into 2027 ([Growbeansprout](https://growbeansprout.com/dbs-ocbc-uob-higher-rates-sep-2026)); (2) Citi-ASEAN monetisation (8.5m customers, wealth income to double by 2030; 1H26 wealth fees +16%); (3) UOB Asset Management sale proceeds. Negative: FY26 fee guidance cut to low-single-digit.

**Cash strength:** CET1 15.4% vs ~11% floor → ~400bp excess; 50% payout (2Q26 interim S$0.88); S$2bn buyback ~40% done (S$794m). No dilution.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal

- **Value-trap check:** Mostly cyclical, with one structural worry: Greater China NPL rose 3.5% → 4.8% in 2Q26 on one real-estate account; HK branch holds 43% share of property loans. Group NPL 1.5%, coverage 88% (306% incl. collateral), credit cost 28bps (guide 25–30).
- **Hidden assets:** UOBAM sale proceeds and excess CET1; no asset exceeds the ~S$69bn market cap.

## 5. Execution & Stress Test

| Case | Target | Upside | Downside to S$39.65 | R:R |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base | S$44 | +1.39 (3.3%) | −2.96 (6.9%) | **0.47 : 1** |

**Fails 3:1** (best case 1.65:1 — the closest of the three). 3:1 needs entry ≈ S$38–39.

**Stress tests:** (1) *Rate reversal:* cuts resume → highest NII sensitivity, ROE slips back toward 11%. (2) *Greater China CRE:* repeat of FY25's S$2.0bn pre-emptive provisioning would cut ROE ~3pp; the most likely stress given 2Q26's NPL jump. (3) *Selloff:* beta to STI; buyback (S$1.2bn left) and 400bp CET1 cushion, ~1.2x P/B ≈ S$36 (−15%).

## 6. Final Verdict

**HOLD** (slight upgrade in tone vs peers). UOB is the cheapest of the three (1.4x P/B vs 2.3–3.2x), the biggest beneficiary of the rate turn and the closest to a 3:1 setup, but ROE recovery is slower, China CRE is an open wound, and consensus is mostly Hold. A decisive close above S$45.20, or a dip to ~S$39–40, would upgrade it to Speculative Buy.
