Latest reported results: 2Q26 (7 Aug 2026). **3Q26 results expected in the second week of November 2026** (date unconfirmed) ([Kopi Notes](https://thekopinotes.com/articles/news/singapore-banks-october-2026-selloff/)).*

## Change since last report (19 Oct)
Price S$41.80 → S$41.09 (-1.7%), now ~3% above the 8–9 Oct low (~S$40.25). Fundamentals unchanged. This brings the stock close to the S$39.5–40.5 level that the 19 Oct report named as an upgrade zone.

## 1. Quality & Growth Filter

- **ROE:** 11.8% (2Q26), the lowest of the three banks. 2Q26 net profit S$1.48bn (+10% YoY); NIM 1.74% vs 1.82% in 1Q26 (a sharper fall than DBS/OCBC) ([Financial Horse](https://financialhorse.com/ocbc-profit-up-22-uob-down-5-which-singapore-bank-would-i-buy-after-record-q2-earnings/), [Kopi Notes](https://thekopinotes.com/articles/news/singapore-banks-october-2026-selloff/)).
- **Inflection:** weak. Profit is growing, but ROE is stagnant in the low double digits and the 2026 fee-income growth guidance was cut.
- **Catalyst (3Q/4Q26):** one-off gain from the UOBAM sale, cost control, and a 3Q print that shows NIM stabilising. Not a strong organic revenue driver.
- **Cash strength:** CET1 ~15.4% (strong), ~4.4% yield; capital returns self-funded, no dilution.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

- **Cyclical or structural?** Mostly cyclical (rates), but Greater China NPLs have risen (group NPL ~1.5%, highest of the three), which is a credit-quality warning rather than a pure cycle effect. Asset turnover/inventory: not meaningful for a bank.
- **Hidden assets:** UOBAM sale proceeds (one-off gain) and the excess CET1 buffer. Nothing exceeds market cap. **Cheap on P/B, but with a reason.**

## 5. Execution & Stress Test

- Entry S$41.09; hard stop **S$39.50** (-3.9%, risk S$1.59, just below the 8 Oct low).
- Base S$44.00: reward S$2.91 → **1.8:1**. Bull S$47.50: reward S$6.41 → **4.0:1**.
- Base case misses the 3:1 minimum; bull clears it.

**Stress tests**
1. **Rates:** NIM at 1.74% is already the weakest; another 5–8bps drop would likely cut FY26 EPS forecasts and push toward S$38 (Citi's target).
2. **Greater China credit:** NPL ratio ~1.5% and rising; a further step-up in Greater China NPLs would likely hit sentiment before earnings.
3. **Market sell-off:** low P/B (1.3x) limits the multiple downside (~1.1x implies ~S$34), and the ~4.4% yield provides some support; this is the most defensive valuation of the three.

## 6. Final Verdict

**HOLD** (upgrade trigger within ~3% of spot). UOB is the cheapest on P/B and yield and has the strongest CET1, but it has the lowest ROE, the weakest NIM trend, rising Greater China NPLs, and a base-case risk/reward of 1.8:1. **Upgrade to Speculative Buy** on a pullback to **S$39.5–40.5**, or after a 3Q print showing NIM stabilising at ≥1.72% and a clean Greater China book. Breakout confirmation above S$43.5. Exit/stop below S$39.50.
