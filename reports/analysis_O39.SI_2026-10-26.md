## 1. Quality & Growth Filter

- **ROE:** 13.7% in 1H26 (vs DBS 17.5%, UOB 11.8%). 1H26 net profit S$4.19bn (+13% YoY); 2Q26 a record S$2.22bn; NIM 1.87% (-2bps QoQ) ([Kopi Notes](https://thekopinotes.com/articles/news/singapore-banks-october-2026-selloff/), [Financial Horse](https://financialhorse.com/ocbc-profit-up-22-uob-down-5-which-singapore-bank-would-i-buy-after-record-q2-earnings/)).
- **Inflection:** earnings and ROE have re-accelerated in 2026 after a flat 2025, helped by wealth/insurance (Great Eastern) contributions. Credit cost guidance FY26: 20–25bps.
- **Catalyst (3Q/4Q26):** fee and insurance income growth, cost discipline, and the 6 Nov print against a depressed bar (Citi: flat YoY).
- **Cash strength:** CET1 ~13.3% (Citi estimate), comfortably above the regulatory minimum; dividends and buybacks funded organically, no dilution. The CET1 buffer is thinner than DBS's.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

- **Cyclical or structural?** NIM compression is rate-driven (sector-wide). NPLs and credit costs are stable; no sign of structural decline. Asset turnover/inventory: not meaningful for a bank.
- **Hidden assets:** the Great Eastern (insurance) holding and Bank of Singapore wealth franchise are carried at book; a sum-of-the-parts uplift is plausible but I found no figure exceeding market cap. **Not a hidden-asset story.**

- Entry S$28.81; hard stop **S$27.50** (-4.5%, risk S$1.31, at the Citi target level).

**Stress tests**

2. **Credit/Greater China:** Singapore/HK/China property and SME exposure; credit cost guidance of 20–25bps has room for upside surprise.
3. **Market sell-off / yield spike:** the stock fell ~10% in two days on 7–8 Oct, the largest of the three; P/B de-rating toward ~1.6x implies ~S$22.

## 6. Final Verdict

**SPECULATIVE BUY (tactical, small size).** Compared with 6 Oct the stock is ~11% cheaper with unchanged fundamentals, brokers' median target (~S$31.7) is ~10% above spot, and the stop is tight and well-defined. But base-case risk/reward is 2.1:1 and the NIM debate is unresolved, so this is not a Strong Buy. Exit on a close below S$27.50; add above S$32. Key checkpoint: 6 Nov results.
