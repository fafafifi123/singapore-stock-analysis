## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

**Value-trap check: still cyclical, not structural.** The NPL ratio is 0.9% and specific allowances are 17bp. Coverage is 124–134% (JPM cites 124%), or ~200% including collateral. Loans are +11% YoY and deposits +10%. The fall in price is driven by market multiple compression and sector de-risking (institutional selling of a crowded, ~26%-of-STI holding), not by deteriorating fundamentals. Asset-turnover and inventory tests do not apply to a bank.

**Hidden assets.** There is no cash pile or land bank larger than the market cap (~S$185bn at S$65.31, derived). The optionality lies in:
- **SRCB:** a 19.9% stake in Shenzhen Rural Commercial Bank, at a cost of ~S$1.8–2.0bn (~S$0.65–0.70/share).
- **Excess CET1:** ~500bp above the requirement on a phased-in basis (more than S$15bn; derived). It funds the unused buyback balance, which is now more value-accretive at a lower price. Some commentators speculate about a special dividend in early 2027 (opinion, not guidance).

P/B is on year-end book value: 1.4, 1.6, 1.4, 1.5, 2.0. The bands are indicative only. ROE was ~11–12% in 2021–22 against ~17% now, so the old mean understates fair value. *EV/EBITDA is not meaningful for a bank*, because deposits are operating raw material, not financing.

**Justified P/B and 2-stage DDM** (BVPS S$24.4):
- **Justified P/B** = (ROE − g)/(COE − g):
  - Conservative: 15% / 3% / 9% → 2.0x, **~S$49**.
  - Base: 17% / 3% / 8.5% → 2.55x, **~S$62**.
  - Aggressive: 18% / 3.5% / 7.5% → 3.6x, **~S$88**.
  - **New sensitivity:** with the US 10-year at ~5.15%, a +50bp COE (base at 9.0%) gives 2.33x, or **~S$57**. Higher discount rates are the main reason bank multiples are compressing, even though higher rates help NII.
- **2-stage DDM** at COE 8.5%. Stage 1: S$3.24 in FY26–27, then S$2.64 ordinary growing 5% to year 5. Stage 2: exit on year-5 BVPS of ~S$29.7.
  - Conservative exit 2.0x: **~S$52**.
  - Base exit 2.55x: **~S$62**.
  - Aggressive exit 3.2x: **~S$75**.
- **Fair-value range: S$49–88, central ~S$62–75.** At S$65.31 the price is near the lower half of the central range. It is closer to fair value than at any point in this report series, but still not cheap versus history.

**6-month target: S$72 (cut from S$74).** The base range is S$70–74: a recovery to the SMA20 (S$70.4) and the 6 Nov absorption zone (S$69–72) once the market stabilises. The bull case is **S$79** (52-week high; JPM S$82). The bear case is **S$58**: another ~10% STI leg at beta ~1, toward the +50bp-COE value of S$57. **Stress tests:**
1. **Rate cuts / SORA reverses:** unlikely in the near term while the Fed is hiking. A US hard landing in 2027 could flip it. SORA at ~1.0% versus the 1.6% plan would mean NII -5% and EPS -5–8%, putting fair P/B at ~2.4x, or **~S$59** (-10%).
2. **Higher-for-longer discount rates (new):** if the US 10-year holds above 5% and COE rises 50bp, value is **~S$57** (-13%), even with NII upside. This is the scenario the market is currently pricing.
3. **Regional credit shock (China/GBA/ASEAN):** specific provisions rising from 17bp to 50bp would cost an extra ~S$1.5bn a year (~-12% EPS), with SRCB impairment risk. Value: **~S$52–55** (-17% to -20%).
4. **Broad market sell-off continues:** the STI is down another 10% at beta ~1, giving **~S$58** (-11%). A full de-rating to the +1 SD P/B band (1.83x) gives **S$45** (-31%), the tail case. The 5.0% yield and the unused ~S$2.6bn buyback are the support.

## 6. Final Verdict

**HOLD. The tactical stop was triggered, so do not add at spot. The core position is unchanged.**

The franchise has not changed:
- NPL is 0.9%.
- Exit NIM is 1.90%, and rates are moving in DBS's favour.
- CET1 is far above requirements.
- JPMorgan has just upgraded the stock.

Valuation has improved: P/B has fallen from 2.85x to 2.68x and the yield is now 5.0%.

The tape is the problem:
- The S$66.40 stop and the SMA200 have both broken.
- Up/down volume has fallen to 0.58 and OBV keeps falling.
- The whole STI is in a high-volatility, rates-driven de-rating.

Base R:R at spot is 1.6:1, below the 3:1 rule.

**Upgrade to Speculative Buy** in either of two cases:
- an entry at **≤S$63.0** with a S$61.20 stop (5:1), or
- a volume-confirmed close back above **S$66.83**, then S$67.80.

**Exit or reassess** on a close below S$61.20.
