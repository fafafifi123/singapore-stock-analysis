## 1. Quality & Growth Filter

**Inflection confirmed:** ROE troughed in FY2025 and has rebuilt for two quarters, driven by fees (wealth fees +42% YoY, wealth AUM >S$500bn) rather than NIM (1.87% in 2Q26, [DBS 2Q26](https://www.dbs.com/newsroom/DBS_second_quarter_net_profit_up_9pct_to_record_SGD_3_08_billion_as_quarterly_total_income_crosses_SGD_6_billion_for_first_time)). ROIC is not meaningful for a bank (deposits are raw material); ROE is the substitute.

**Catalysts for 3Q/4Q26:** (1) rollout of 18 new wealth centres from 3Q26; (2) the Sep Fed hike — DBS discloses ~S$11m NII per bp of SGD rates ([Kopi Notes](https://thekopinotes.com/articles/news/singapore-banks-dbs-ocbc-uob-fed-rate-hike-q4-2026/)), so +25bp ≈ S$275m annualised, though benefits lag and may not show until 2027 ([Growbeansprout](https://growbeansprout.com/dbs-ocbc-uob-higher-rates-sep-2026)); (3) MAS October policy statement.

**Cash strength (FCF substitute):** CET1 16.6% transitional / 14.6% fully phased vs ~9% MAS minimum → ~560–760bp excess; funds an S$8bn capital-return programme (S$3bn buyback + S$0.15/qtr) with zero dilution.

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal

- **Value-trap check:** Not a trap — NPL 1.0%, specific allowances 15bps of loans, loans +8% YoY. NIM compression was cyclical; the rate hike marks a possible turn. Asset turnover/inventory are not applicable to a bank.
- **Hidden assets:** 19.9% of Shenzhen Rural Commercial Bank (~S$1.8–2.0bn cost, ~1.4% of market cap — immaterial to valuation) and the excess-capital buffer. Nothing exceeds market cap (~S$220bn). Premises revaluation surplus: data not found.

## 6. Final Verdict

**HOLD.** Quality is real (ROE inflection, clean credit, self-funded capital return) and the Fed hike is a modest new tailwind, but the stock already trades at ~3.2x book at consensus target, with sub-1:1 risk/reward. Hold for yield; add on a pullback to ~S$70.
