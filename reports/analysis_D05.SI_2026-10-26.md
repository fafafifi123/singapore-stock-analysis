## 1. Quality & Growth Filter

| Period | Net profit | ROE |
|---|---|---|
| FY2023 | S$10.3bn | 18.0% |
| FY2024 | S$11.4bn | 18.0% |
| FY2025 | S$11.0bn (global minimum tax) | 16.2% (trough) |
| 1H26 / 2Q26 | S$6.01bn record | 17.5% / 17.9% |

**Inflection: yes.** ROE troughed in FY2025 and recovered for two quarters, led by fee income rather than NIM (2Q26 net fees +25% YoY; wealth fees +42%; wealth AUM >S$500bn). NIM fell to 1.87% in 2Q26 ([Growbeansprout](https://growbeansprout.com/dbs-2q26-profit-dividend)).

**Catalyst for 3Q/4Q26:** wealth-centre rollout (18 new centres, first wave from 3Q26), treasury customer and markets income, and FY26 guidance already raised. The risk to this catalyst is that wealth fees are market-sensitive, which is why the October equity/bond sell-off matters.

**Cash strength (bank substitute for FCF):** CET1 16.6% transitional / 14.6% fully phased-in vs a 9.0% MAS minimum, i.e. roughly 560–760bps excess. This funds the S$8bn capital-return programme (S$3bn buyback plus S$0.15/quarter capital-return dividends to FY2027) with no dilution. Forward dividend yield ~4.3% (2026) per CGS International ([AsiaOne](https://www.asiaone.com/money/singapore-banks-stocks-drop-dbs-uob-ocbc-share-price)).

## 2. Deep Value & Reversal Assessment

- **Value-trap check:** NPL ratio ~1.0%, specific allowances ~15bps of loans, coverage 130%. NIM compression is rate-cycle driven and decelerating (-2bps QoQ in 2Q26), not a structural franchise decline. Asset turnover and inventory are not meaningful for a bank; loans +8% YoY and deposits +11% YoY show a growing balance sheet.
- **Hidden assets:** 19.9% stake in Shenzhen Rural Commercial Bank (cumulative cost ~S$1.8–2.0bn), small against a ~S$210bn market cap, so not a market-cap-exceeding asset. Market value of owned premises: not found.
- **Conclusion:** quality compounder, not a deep-value reversal. Nothing is hidden.

- **The base case does not meet the 3:1 minimum.** Only the bull case does.

**Stress tests**
1. **Rate cuts resume / yields spike:** a renewed cutting cycle reopens NIM compression (SGD book ~S$11m per bp). A yield spike hits wealth AUM and fee income, the engine of the ROE recovery. Bear case ~S$66.
2. **Greater China credit shock:** the SRCB stake and HK/China exposure are the channels. Specific allowances could move from 15bps toward 40bps+; the CET1 buffer absorbs it but buybacks would likely slow.
3. **Broad market sell-off:** DBS is ~26% of the STI. Reversion to the 5-yr avg P/B (~1.9x) implies ~S$45 on unchanged book, a tail scenario. Buybacks stepped up in past dips and the ~4% yield cushions the fall.

## 6. Final Verdict

**HOLD.** Fundamentals are improving (ROE 17.5%, record fees, clean credit, self-funded capital return) but the stock still trades at ~3.1x book vs a ~1.9x five-year average, brokers' central targets are within ~5–10% of spot, and base-case risk/reward is ~1.2:1. Accumulate on a pullback to **S$70–71**; breakout buy only on a close above **S$78.5**. Re-rate after the 5 Nov results.
