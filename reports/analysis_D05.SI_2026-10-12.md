| Period | ROE | Note |

**Inflection confirmed:** ROE troughed in FY2025 and has risen for two consecutive quarters ([DBS 2Q26 CFO deck](https://www.dbs.com/iwov-resources/images/investors/quarterly-financials/2026/2Q26_CFO_presentation.pdf)). ROIC is not meaningful for a bank; ROE is the capital-efficiency proxy.

**Catalyst, next 2 quarters:** fee income (wealth fees +42% YoY in 2Q26; AUM >S$500bn; 18 new wealth centres from 3Q26) and — new since August — a **rising-rate tailwind**: management's FY26 guidance assumed no further Fed cuts, and the Fed has since hiked. Guidance was already raised (total income to exceed 2025) ([The Smart Investor](https://thesmartinvestor.com.sg/?p=99676)). NIM was 1.87% in 2Q26; a higher-for-longer curve argues the NIM decline is ending.

**Cash strength (capital-generation proxy):** CET1 ~16.6% transitional vs. ~9% MAS minimum; funds S$8bn capital return (S$3bn buyback + S$0.15/qtr capital-return dividend through FY27) with no dilution. FY26E dividend yield **4.5%**, FY27E **5.0%** (FactSet, via Growbeansprout).

| Case | Target | Reward | R:R |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | S$78 | +S$4.15 | **0.7 : 1** |

**Fails 3:1.** A 3:1 requires entry ≈ **S$72.5** (bull) or ≈ S$70.5 (base). Accumulation zone: S$70–72.5.

**Stress tests:**
1. **Global bank sell-off extends (another ~−12%):** de-rating to ~2.5x P/B ⇒ ≈S$64; buyback acceleration and a >5% FY27E yield partly cushion. Estimated −13% from here.
2. **Fed pivots back to cuts:** reopens NIM compression (DBS SGD-book sensitivity ≈ S$11m/bp); fee growth must carry earnings. Fair value drifts toward S$65–70.
3. **Regional credit/geopolitical shock (GBA/China, oil-driven inflation):** allowances could rise from ~15bp toward 40bp+; ~560bp CET1 buffer absorbs it, buyback pauses. Downside S$58–62.
Upside scenario: yields stay high, NII guidance lifted, S$80–86 retest.

## 6. Final Verdict

**HOLD** (bias: accumulate below S$72.5). Quality and ROE inflection are intact and the sell-off improved entry by ~5%, but at 2.87x P/B (vs. ~1.9x 5-yr average) it is not a hidden gem, and base-case reward/risk is <1:1. Not a value trap.
